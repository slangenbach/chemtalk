# Generative AI for a Voice System

## Business Context:

A large share of the workforce operates hands-busy or eyes-busy; on the plant floor, in the lab, driving between
sites, or wearing protective equipment, and cannot comfortably type into the main corporate chat assistant. Today they
either stop what they are doing to use a keyboard, wait until they are back at a desk, or skip the query altogether.
This limits how and when people reach the information they need, and it excludes roles where typing is impractical from
the assistant's benefits.

## Problem to Solve:

Develop an internal system that lets users interact with the corporate assistant by voice: speak a question and hear (or
read) the answer back. The solution should handle spoken input reliably in realistic working conditions and respond
quickly enough to feel conversational.

## Presentation:

You will be given 30 minutes to present your results followed by 30 minutes of Q&A.
Please present your results and outline possible next steps to productionize the solution.

## Note:

The use of AI in any form or format is allowed and encouraged, since it reflects real working conditions. You should
fully own and be able to defend everything in your deliverable.
